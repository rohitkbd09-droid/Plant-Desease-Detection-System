# ==================== KEY ENDPOINTS ====================

POST /predict
  Description: Analyze plant disease with severity
  Input: image file, crop_type, location, lat/lon (optional)
  Output: disease, confidence, severity (mild/moderate/severe), affected_area_%

GET /calendar/crop/<crop_name>
  Description: Get farming calendar
  Output: sowing dates, fertilizer schedule, irrigation plan

POST /weather
  Description: Get weather and disease risk
  Input: latitude, longitude, disease, crop_type
  Output: weather data, disease risk level, recommendations

GET /dashboard/<user_id>
  Description: Get health analytics
  Output: total_scans, disease_frequency, recovery_rate, trends

GET /report/pdf/<scan_id>
  Description: Download PDF report
  Output: PDF file

# ==================== DISEASE SEVERITY CLASSES ====================

MILD (< 15% affected):
  - Minor leaf discoloration
  - Few spots or patches
  - No urgent action needed
  - Preventive measures sufficient

MODERATE (15-40% affected):
  - Multiple disease signs
  - Visible lesions
  - Treatment recommended within 7 days
  - Monitor closely

SEVERE (> 40% affected):
  - Widespread infection
  - Large affected areas
  - Immediate action required
  - Increase pesticide frequency


# ==================== CROP CALENDAR EVENTS ====================

Rice (Kharif Season - May to November):
  May: Field Prep + Sowing
  June: Weeding + Fertilizer
  July-Aug: Pest Surveillance
  Sep: Disease Surveillance
  Oct-Nov: Harvest

Wheat (Rabi Season - November to May):
  Oct: Land Prep
  Nov: Sowing
  Dec-Feb: Irrigation (multiple stages)
  Mar: Disease Surveillance
  Apr-May: Harvest

Cotton (Full Year - April to November):
  Apr: Sowing
  May-Jun: Weeding
  Jul-Aug: Fertilizer + Pest Watch
  Sep-Nov: Harvesting


# ==================== RECOMMENDED PESTICIDES ====================

Blight:
  - Copper Oxychloride (2 g/L)
  - Tricyclazole (0.6 g/L)
  - Spray every 7-10 days

Rust:
  - Sulfur Dust
  - Potassium fungicide
  - Apply at first sign

Powdery Mildew:
  - Neem Oil
  - Sulfur fungicide
  - Spray late afternoon

Leaf Blight:
  - Hexaconazole (1.25 mL/L)
  - Kasugamycin (3 mL/L)
  - Weekly application
