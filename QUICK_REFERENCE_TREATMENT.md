# PLANT Disease Treatment - Quick Reference Card

## **BLIGHT** - Early Blight Fungal Infection
**SEVERITY**: 🔴 HIGH - ACT IMMEDIATELY  
**RECOVERY**: 2-3 weeks with treatment

### ⚡ EMERGENCY STEPS (Do First!)
1. **CUT & BURN** - Remove all brown-spotted leaves NOW. Burn or bury deep. NEVER compost.
2. **SPRAY TODAY** - Prepare copper fungicide spray

### 🧪 TREATMENT OPTIONS

| PRIMARY | ALTERNATIVE 1 | ALTERNATIVE 2 | ORGANIC |
|---------|---------------|---------------|---------|
| Copper Oxychloride 50% WP 2-2.5 g/L | Chlorothalonil 75% WP 2-2.5 g/L | Mancozeb 75% WP 1.5-2 g/L | Neem Oil 5 ml/L |
| **REPEAT**: Every 7 days | - | - | - |
| **SPRAYS**: 2-3 times | - | - | - |

### 💊 FERTILIZER (24-48 hrs after spray)
- **NPK 19:19:19** - 5 g per liter
- **ALTERNATE**: Calcium Nitrate + Potassium Sulfate

### 🛠️ EQUIPMENT NEEDED
- Knapsack sprayer (16-20 L)
- Protective gloves, mask, boots
- Pruning shears (sterilized)
- Measuring scale

### ✅ SUCCESS SIGNS
- ✓ Lesions turn brown
- ✓ Yellow halos disappear  
- ✓ New leaves without spots
- ✓ Leaf drop slows

### ⚠️ KEY PRECAUTIONS
- Do NOT use overhead watering during treatment
- Wear full protective gear
- Keep 7-14 day harvest interval
- Never mix with oil-based products

## **RUST** - Fungal Rust Infection
**SEVERITY**: 🟠 MEDIUM-HIGH - Act Now  
**RECOVERY**: 1-2 weeks with treatment

### ⚡ EMERGENCY STEPS (Do First!)
1. **REMOVE RUSTY LEAVES** - Cut off leaves with >50% orange pustules
2. **SPRAY UNDERSIDE** - Rust hides on leaf bottom

### 🧪 TREATMENT OPTIONS

| PRIMARY | ALTERNATIVE 1 | ALTERNATIVE 2 | ORGANIC |
|---------|---------------|---------------|---------|
| Sulfur 80% WDG 2 g/L | Tebuconazole 25% EC 1 ml/2L | Hexaconazole 5% EC 2 ml/L | Neem + Sulfur 5+2 ml/g per L |
| **REPEAT**: Every 7-10 days | - | - | - |
| **SPRAYS**: 2-3 times | - | - | - |

### 💊 FERTILIZER (1-2 days after spray)
- **SOP (Potassium Sulfate) 0:0:50** - 3-5 g per liter
- **ALTERNATE**: Monopotassium Phosphate (MKP) 4-6 g/L

### 🛠️ EQUIPMENT NEEDED
- Backpack sprayer
- Protective suit, gloves, mask
- Pruning shears
- Measuring scale

### ✅ SUCCESS SIGNS
- ✓ Orange pustules stop growing
- ✓ Pustules turn dark brown
- ✓ No new yellow spots
- ✓ New leaves healthy

### ⚠️ KEY PRECAUTIONS
- Apply sulfur ONLY when temp < 85°F
- Do NOT use oil spray within 2 weeks of sulfur
- Use DRIP watering (no overhead)
- Sterilize all tools

---

## **POWDERY MILDEW** - Fungal Infection
**SEVERITY**: 🟡 MEDIUM - Act Promptly  
**RECOVERY**: 1-2 weeks with treatment

### ⚡ EMERGENCY STEPS (Do First!)
1. **PRUNE HEAVILY** - Remove leaves with >60% white powder
2. **INCREASE AIRFLOW** - Prune lower branches
3. **STOP NITROGEN** - Can accelerate mildew

### 🧪 TREATMENT OPTIONS

| PRIMARY | ALTERNATIVE 1 | ALTERNATIVE 2 | ORGANIC |
|---------|---------------|---------------|---------|
| Potassium Bicarbonate 5 g/L | Sulfur 80% WP 2-2.5 g/L | Trichoderma (Bio) per label | Baking Soda 1 tbsp/L + soap |
| **OR**: Neem Oil 3 ml/L | - | - | - |
| **REPEAT**: Every 5-7 days | - | - | - |
| **SPRAYS**: 2-4 times | - | - | - |

### 💊 FERTILIZER (2-3 days after spray)
- **⚠️ IMPORTANT**: REDUCE or STOP nitrogen fertilizer
- Use: **Potassium Nitrate (KNO3)** - 3-5 g per liter
- **ALTERNATE**: Calcium + Potassium mix

### 🛠️ EQUIPMENT NEEDED
- Knapsack sprayer  
- Protective gloves and mask
- Pruning shears
- Measuring scale
- Spray timer

### ✅ SUCCESS SIGNS
- ✓ White powder disappears
- ✓ Leaves green again
- ✓ No new white patches
- ✓ Leaf curling stops

### ⚠️ KEY PRECAUTIONS
- Apply spray LATE AFTERNOON (not midday sun = leaf burn)
- REDUCE nitrogen completely during treatment
- Do NOT mix sulfur + oil within 2 weeks
- Keep humidity 40-60% if possible

## **HEALTHY PLANT** - Maintenance Mode
**SEVERITY**: ✅ Green Light  
**MONITORING**: Ongoing

### 📋 MAINTENANCE CHECKLIST
- Inspect leaves every 3-4 days (especially undersides)
- Water at soil level, avoid overhead
- Remove dead/yellow leaves first seen
- Sanitize tools between plants
- Keep area clean (no fallen leaves)

### 💊 ROUTINE FEEDING
- **Balanced Fertilizer** - NPK 10:10:10, apply every 20-25 days
- **Compost** - 1-2 kg per plant every 2-3 weeks

### 🛑 EARLY WARNING SIGNS
- ⚠️ Any brown spots? → Check for BLIGHT
- ⚠️ Orange powder on leaves? → Check for RUST
- ⚠️ White powder on leaves? → Check for POWDERY MILDEW
- ⚠️ Any of above symptoms → Use disease-specific treatment

## SPRAY SCHEDULE TEMPLATE

### Week 1-2: INTENSIVE TREATMENT
| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Day 1 | Cut infected leaves + First spray (Pesticide) |
| Day 3 | Monitor (no spraying) |
| Day 5 | Monitor (no spraying) |
| Day 7 | Second spray (Pesticide) + Fertilizer (if 48 hrs passed) |
| Day 9-10 | Monitor (look for signs) |

### Week 2-3: CONTINUE TREATMENT
- Repeat pesticide spray every 7-10 days (Rust/Blight) or every 5-7 days (Mildew)
- Apply fertilizer every 10-12 days
- Scout for new symptoms every 3 days

### Week 3+: TRANSITION TO MAINTENANCE
- Once 7-10 disease-free days pass, reduce spray frequency
- Switch to preventive fertilization only
- Return to routine monitoring

## QUICK DOSAGE REFERENCE

### PESTICIDES (mixing in water)
```
1 Liter spray:
- Copper Oxychloride: 2-2.5 grams
- Chlorothalonil: 2-2.5 grams  
- Sulfur: 2 grams
- Potassium Bicarbonate: 5 grams
- Neem Oil: 3 ml + 1 ml soap
- Baking Soda: 1 tablespoon + 5 ml soap

20 Liter sprayer:
- Multiply above by 20
```

### FERTILIZERS (mixing in water)
```
1 Liter spray:
- NPK 19:19:19: 5 grams
- SOP 0:0:50: 3-5 grams
- KNO3: 3-5 grams
- MKP: 4-6 grams

20 Liter sprayer:
- Multiply above by 20
```

## WHEN TO CALL AN EXPERT

❌ Disease getting worse despite treatment  
❌ Multiple different diseases on same plant  
❌ Unknown symptoms not in this guide  
❌ Plant dying rapidly  
❌ Cannot identify disease from symptoms  
❌ Treatment causing plant damage  

**CALL**: Local Agricultural Extension Office or Agronomist

---

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Mixing random pesticides** - Can damage plants  
❌ **Exceeding recommended dosage** - Wastes money, harms plant  
❌ **Spraying in midday sun** - Causes leaf burn  
❌ **Using overhead watering during treatment** - Spreads disease faster  
❌ **Not following harvest interval** - Food safety risk  
❌ **Composting infected leaves** - Spreads disease to other areas  
❌ **Spraying without protective gear** - Health hazard  
❌ **Giving up too early** - Diseases need consistent treatment
